Web3Edu — Applied Research Infrastructure for Verifiable Learning

Web3Edu is an applied research and learning ecosystem built on a live Hyperledger Besu network. It connects hands-on technical activity, blockchain interaction, learner identity, and verifiable progress records to support evidence-backed education in Web3 and cybersecurity.

The ecosystem combines structured labs, wallet-based interaction, identity credentials, smart contract exercises, community experimentation, and governance-oriented learning paths.
